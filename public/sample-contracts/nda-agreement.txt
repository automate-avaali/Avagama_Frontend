MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of 10 January 2026 ("Effective Date") between Adventure Works Ltd. ("Party A") and Wingtip Toys Pvt. Ltd. ("Party B").

1. Purpose
The parties wish to exchange confidential information for the purpose of evaluating a potential business relationship.

2. Confidentiality
Each party shall keep the other party's Confidential Information secret and use it solely for the Purpose. The confidentiality obligations shall survive for three (3) years after termination.

3. Intellectual Property
Each party retains ownership of its own pre-existing intellectual property. No license is granted under this Agreement except as strictly necessary for the Purpose.

4. Limitation of Liability
Each party's aggregate liability under this Agreement is capped at the fees or consideration exchanged in the twelve (12) months preceding a claim.

5. Indemnification
Each party shall indemnify the other, on a mutual basis, against claims arising from its own breach of confidentiality.

6. Warranties
Each party represents and warrants that it has full authority to enter into this Agreement.

7. Term and Termination
Either party may terminate this Agreement on thirty (30) days' written notice, or immediately for a material breach that is not cured within thirty (30) days of notice.

8. Payment Terms
Any fees payable under this Agreement shall be due within thirty (30) days of the invoice date (Net 30).

9. Auto-Renewal
This Agreement renews for successive one-year terms unless either party gives sixty (60) days' written notice before the renewal date.

10. Governing Law and Venue
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of England and Wales, and the courts of London shall have exclusive jurisdiction.

11. Force Majeure
Neither party shall be liable for any delay or failure caused by events beyond its reasonable control.

12. Data Privacy
Each party shall comply with applicable data protection laws, and a Data Processing Addendum applies where personal data is shared.

13. Dispute Resolution and Audit
The parties shall resolve disputes through good-faith negotiation and then binding arbitration, and each party may, on reasonable prior notice, audit the other's compliance with this Agreement.

14. Insurance
Each party shall maintain insurance coverage adequate to its obligations under this Agreement.

15. Assignment
Neither party may assign this Agreement without the other party's prior written consent.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have executed this Agreement as of the Effective Date.
