SOFTWARE AS A SERVICE SUBSCRIPTION AGREEMENT

This Software as a Service Subscription Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of 15 February 2026 ("Effective Date") between Fabrikam Cloud Inc. ("Provider") and Tailspin Retail Pvt. Ltd. ("Customer").

1. Subscription and Access
The Provider grants the Customer a non-exclusive, non-transferable subscription to access the hosted software during the subscription term.

2. Confidentiality
Each party shall protect the other party's Confidential Information and use it only to perform under this Agreement, with obligations surviving for three (3) years after termination.

3. Intellectual Property
The Provider retains all rights, title and interest in the software and related materials. The Customer retains all rights in its own data.

4. Limitation of Liability
Each party's aggregate liability under this Agreement is capped at the total fees paid by the Customer in the twelve (12) months preceding the event giving rise to the claim.

5. Indemnification
The Provider shall indemnify the Customer against third-party claims alleging that the software infringes their intellectual property rights.

6. Warranties
The Provider warrants that the software will materially conform to its published documentation during the subscription term.

7. Term and Termination
Either party may terminate this Agreement for material breach if the breach is not cured within thirty (30) days of written notice.

8. Payment Terms
The Customer shall pay all subscription fees within sixty (60) days of the invoice date (Net 60).

9. Auto-Renewal
The subscription renews automatically for successive annual terms unless either party provides thirty (30) days' written notice before the renewal date.

10. Governing Law
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of New York.

11. Force Majeure
Neither party shall be liable for any failure or delay caused by events beyond its reasonable control.

12. Data Privacy
The Provider shall process personal data in accordance with applicable data protection laws and the Data Processing Addendum attached to this Agreement.

13. Dispute Resolution
Disputes shall be resolved through good-faith negotiation between the parties and, failing resolution, by binding arbitration.

14. Insurance
The Provider shall maintain insurance coverage consistent with industry standards for a provider of hosted software services.
