MASTER VENDOR SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Vendor Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of 1 March 2026 ("Effective Date") by and between Contoso Systems Inc., a company with its registered office at 100 Market Street, San Francisco, CA ("Client"), and Northwind Supplies Pvt. Ltd., a company with its registered office at 42 MG Road, Bengaluru, India ("Vendor").

1. Scope of Services
The Vendor shall provide software development and support services as described in each Statement of Work agreed between the parties.

2. Confidentiality
Each party shall keep the other party's Confidential Information secret and use it only to perform its obligations under this Agreement. The confidentiality obligations shall survive termination for a period of three (3) years.

3. Intellectual Property
All work product created by the Vendor under this Agreement, AND all of the Vendor's pre-existing intellectual property, tools and libraries used in connection with the services, shall be exclusively assigned to and owned by the Client upon creation. The Vendor retains no rights whatsoever in its pre-existing materials.

4. Limitation of Liability
The Vendor's liability under this Agreement shall be unlimited. There shall be no cap on the Vendor's aggregate liability for any claims, damages or losses of any kind, including indirect, incidental and consequential damages.

5. Indemnification
The Vendor shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless the Client from any and all claims, losses and liabilities arising out of or in connection with this Agreement. The Client shall have no indemnification obligations to the Vendor.

6. Warranties
The Vendor warrants that the services will be performed in a professional and workmanlike manner consistent with industry standards.

7. Term and Termination
The Client may terminate this Agreement immediately for any breach by the Vendor, with no obligation to provide prior notice or any opportunity to cure. The Vendor may not terminate this Agreement for convenience under any circumstances.

8. Payment Terms
The Client shall pay all undisputed invoices within ninety (90) days of receipt (Net 90).

9. Auto-Renewal
This Agreement automatically renews for successive one-year terms unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least five (5) days before the end of the then-current term.

10. Governing Law and Venue
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of California, and the parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts located in San Francisco County.

11. Force Majeure
Neither party shall be liable for delays or failures caused by events beyond its reasonable control.

12. Data Privacy
The Vendor shall comply with applicable data protection laws when processing personal data on the Client's behalf.

13. Dispute Resolution
The parties shall attempt to resolve disputes in good faith; failing which, disputes shall be referred to binding arbitration.

14. Insurance
The Vendor shall maintain commercial general liability insurance of not less than USD 1,000,000.

15. Assignment
The Client may assign this Agreement freely to any third party. The Vendor may not assign this Agreement without the Client's prior written consent.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have executed this Agreement as of the Effective Date.
