Sample contracts for the Contract Clause Review Agent tutorial.

These are FICTIONAL, synthetic demo contracts (fictional parties such as Contoso,
Northwind, Fabrikam, Tailspin, Adventure Works, Wingtip Toys). They exist only to
demonstrate the agent's three routing outcomes in the Agent Solutions walkthrough:

  high-risk-vendor-agreement.txt  -> High risk   (uncapped liability, one-sided IP
                                                  assignment, no cure period) -> legal review
  saas-agreement.txt              -> Medium risk (Net 60 payment terms, missing
                                                  audit-rights clause) -> auto-approved, flagged
  nda-agreement.txt               -> Low risk    (clean mutual NDA, standard clauses)
                                                  -> auto-approved

Not real agreements. Do not use for any actual contracting.
